Fashion Discovery Platform for Nepal

Idea Overview

The proposed application is a fashion discovery platform that brings products from different online clothing businesses in Nepal into one place. Currently, customers may need to browse different websites, Instagram pages, and online stores individually to find the clothing they want. This platform would allow users to search and compare fashion products from multiple sellers through a single application. Users could search based on factors such as budget, size, color, style, gender, and product category. The platform would also provide festival and occasion-based fashion discovery, such as outfits for Dashain, Tihar, Teej, weddings, parties, college events, and office wear. For example, a user could search for a "Dashain outfit under Rs. 5,000" and receive suitable products or outfit combinations from different Nepali online clothing stores.

Problem Statement

Nepal has a growing number of online clothing businesses, but these businesses are spread across individual websites, social media pages, and different e-commerce platforms. Because there is no fashion-focused platform that brings these options together, customers may have to spend significant time visiting multiple stores to search for products, check prices, compare styles, and find something within their budget. General e-commerce platforms offer fashion products but are not primarily designed around fashion discovery, styling, festivals, occasions, and personalized clothing preferences. Therefore, there is an opportunity to create a centralized fashion discovery platform that makes it easier for customers to find and compare clothing available from different online businesses in Nepal.

Objectives

The main objective of the application is to simplify fashion discovery and connect customers with multiple online clothing businesses in Nepal through a single platform. The application aims to:
Bring fashion products from different Nepali online clothing stores into one searchable platform.
Allow customers to compare products from different sellers without visiting each store individually.
Help users find clothing according to their budget, size, color, style, category, and other preferences.
Provide festival and occasion-based fashion discovery, including Dashain, Tihar, Teej, weddings, parties, college events, and office wear.
Allow users to set a budget and receive suitable product or complete outfit suggestions within that budget.
Help small and growing Nepali online fashion businesses increase their visibility and reach potential customers.
In future versions, introduce features such as personalized recommendations and image-based search, where users could upload an outfit or clothing image and find similar products available from Nepali sellers.
The overall goal is not to create another general marketplace, but to create a specialized platform that makes discovering fashion across Nepal's many online clothing businesses easier, faster, and more personalized.
